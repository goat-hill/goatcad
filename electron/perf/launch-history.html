Launch performance

Recorded by ./bin/launch-timing --record, from electron/. Every row is the same method: a fresh build launched with a fresh profile and no files; one warm-up launch is discarded, then the row holds the median of 10 launches. Times are ms since the main process started (milestones in ARCHITECTURE.md §3.1). Headless runs under xvfb with software GL; desktop runs (--visible) use the real GPU and compositor. Rows compare only within one machine and one display mode, so each pair has its own table, newest first. A * on a commit means the app's sources differed from it; the label says how. Release rows are labeled with the version; a branch's rows carry its name and may be pruned when it lands.
