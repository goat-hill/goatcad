GoatCAD™ and the goat brand mark are trademarks of Goat Hill Electronics LLC. The GPL covers
GoatCAD's code; it grants no rights to the name or the mark.

**Fine without asking:**

- Saying your work runs with, connects to, or is a plug-in for GoatCAD, as long as it does not
  suggest Goat Hill Electronics made or endorses it.
- Writing about GoatCAD: articles, tutorials, videos, reviews.
- Redistributing unmodified official builds under their own name.

**Ask first:**

- Distributing a modified build under the GoatCAD name or with its logo. A fork must use a
  different name and mark, and its own app identity (`appId`, update URL).
- Using the name or mark in a product, company, service or domain name.

Questions go to the maintainers through a GitHub issue.
